# Data audit

| Accession | GEO platform | Curated comparison | Important note |
|---|---|---|---|
| GSE65194 | GPL570 | TNBC vs normal breast | 55 TNBC arrays represent 41 biological tumors; technical replicates are present. |
| GSE45827 | GPL570 | basal/TNBC vs normal breast | Same Institut Curie specimens as GSE65194; not an independent cohort. |
| GSE36295 | GPL6244 | TNBC vs normal breast | TNBC status is selected from the deposited phenotype field. |

The article assigns 11 TNBC/5 normal to GSE45827 and 41 TNBC/11 normal to GSE36295. GEO records indicate these counts are reversed. The project preserves a paper-style analysis and a corrected analysis.

The serum LC-MS raw data and sample-level intensity table are not publicly deposited. Metabolite discovery, OPLS-DA, ROC recalculation and DeLong testing cannot be independently reproduced from Table 1 alone.
